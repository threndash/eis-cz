Czechia in the European Innovation Scoreboard

The view of the EIS data follows the logic of tracking trends in the individual EIS indicators, in order to identify the areas where Czechia lags behind the most and where such lagging may be at risk. A combination of two measures serves this purpose. The first is the SII, or rather its composite indicators: we track the EU level in 2019 and how the positions of Czechia and the EU change against this baseline over a time series of 8 years. The largest differences indicate areas of lagging, and the last year (2026) serves primarily to identify them. For each year the difference in levels between the EU and Czechia is calculated, and convergence expresses the net score of the differences over the 8-year period. It shows by how many percentage points Czechia moves closer to, or further away from, the EU in this period.

5 groups of indicators were identified, and their characteristics also imply the prioritisation and the basic goal. As context, basic information on each indicator (what it measures) is provided, together with information on the data inputs in terms of the years in which they were measured (many indicators have a substantial time lag between measurement and inclusion in the EIS).
